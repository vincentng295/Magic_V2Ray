Magic V2Ray

System-wide proxy for rooted Android, powered by Xray-core.

Module version:

Magic V2Ray runs as a root daemon and marks traffic at the Netfilter layer, so every app — system UIDs and hotspot clients included — goes through your proxy without taking Android's single VPN slot.
